About Us

A warm table in the heart of Dutse

Hasinah Confectionery and Restaurant is a restaurant and confectionery in Dutse, Jigawa State, offering meals, snacks, drinks and confectionery with dine-in, takeaway and delivery options.

Whether you are grabbing a quick meat pie on the way to work, sitting down to a proper meal with family and friends, or ordering fresh food to your door, our team is ready to serve you. Everything — from shawarma and crispy chicken to cakes and fresh juice — is made at our own kitchen on Nuhu Muhammadu Sanusi Way.
